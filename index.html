I am a Senior Economist at the Burning Glass Institute. I completed my PhD at the Paris School of Economics (PSE), where I worked on public economics, labor economics and econometrics. My research covers the effect of fixed-term contracts on the wage structure, a new, more efficient estimator for instrumental variable settings, and public discourse about welfare benefits. Before I converted to economics, I was a physicist. You can find my research here and my resume here.

In my free time, I climb, cook and play music, among other things.
