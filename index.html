Simon Löwe

I am an economist at the Burning Glass Institute. I completed my PhD at the Paris School of Economics (PSE), where I worked on public economics, labor economics and econometrics. I studied the effect of media stigmatization on benefit take-up, the effect of short-term contracts on the wage structure and proposed a new more efficient estimator in an Instrumental Variable setting. You can find my research here.

Before I converted to economics, I was a physicist. You can find my resume here.
